Pricing Physical Climate Risk: Acute Risk and Chronic Risk

Sole-authored · November 2026

I aggregate county-level climate signals across firms' operating establishments and study how U.S. equity returns relate differently to acute disaster losses and chronic physical exposure.

Paper (PDF)

This paper examines the relation between physical climate exposure and equity returns, distinguishing acute disaster losses from chronic exposure. Monthly county-level climate signals are aggregated across firms' establishments using annual sales and employment weights and linked to U.S. publicly listed firms over 1990–2022. Chronic exposure predicts higher subsequent returns across the reported horizons and remains informative after conventional firm controls and alternative return benchmarks. Acute exposure has weaker and less stable predictive power. The chronic-exposure slope is also larger among small firms, consistent with greater difficulty processing long-run climate information. Climate-exposure characteristics predict subsequent returns, whereas climate-beta prices are statistically insignificant.

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Team TAKE

Untethered Health Monitor

Space health · NASA open data · Interactive simulation

How can a crew respond to health and environmental warnings when help from Earth is delayed? Our team built an interactive spacecraft and Mars rover prototype that connects sensor readings, crew self-checks, response tasks, and delayed ground messages.

My contribution. I developed the scientific evidence and data foundation: reviewing NASA sources, preparing and checking environmental and radiation datasets, documenting assumptions, and developing scenario and presentation materials.
